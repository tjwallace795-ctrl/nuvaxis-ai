Our Story

One family, one kitchen, since 2009.

La Feta started as a Sunday-dinner habit that got out of hand. Yiayia Sofia's recipes, a tiny storefront in Deep Ellum, and a stubborn belief that good food takes time. Fifteen years later the phyllo is still rolled by hand and the grill still runs on olive wood.

We source what we can from small producers — feta and oil from family farms in the Peloponnese, produce from the Dallas Farmers Market. Nothing frozen, nothing rushed.

Made from scratch

Phyllo, sauces, dressings, and desserts — all in-house, every morning.

Family recipes

The same dishes three generations grew up eating in Kalamata.

Wood-fired grill

Olive-wood coals for the char you can't fake on a flat-top.

Everyone's welcome

Vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free eaters always have a full table.
