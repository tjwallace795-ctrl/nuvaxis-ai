About Wallace Roofing

Roofing you can trust because we live here too.

Wallace Roofing Co. LLC is a locally owned, family-operated roofing contractor based at 145 W Neagle Avenue in Paris, Texas. We started on a handshake, and we still run that way today.

Our customers don't get passed off to a sales team. When you call, you talk to the same family that climbs the ladder. That's why forty Lamar County homeowners have given us a perfect five-star rating, and why most of our work comes from people we've already taken care of recommending us to their neighbors.

We handle everything from asphalt shingle replacements on single-family homes to flat-roof commercial systems on warehouses and churches. Whether it's storm damage, an active leak, or a roof that's simply reached the end of its life, we show up, give you a straight answer, and do the work right the first time.
